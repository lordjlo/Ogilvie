# Employee Intelligence Brief: Ogilvie Fleet
**Date:** April 2026 | **Period:** 2023–April 2026 | **Focus:** Strategic capability, culture health, EV/tech execution readiness

---

## Executive Summary

Ogilvie Fleet is a culturally stable, growing independent UK fleet lessor (150+ employees, ~£247M turnover, 22,000+ on-risk vehicles) that has just completed its most significant leadership transition in 26 years, replacing a founding-era MD with an internal promotion backed by a deliberate restructure. The company has achieved Best Companies "Outstanding" accreditation for six consecutive years, signals near-zero attrition, and is actively investing in technology, AI, and mobility ecosystem expansion — including the December 2025 acquisition of ONWRD. Hiring has been concentrated in salary sacrifice, daily rental, and south-of-England geographic expansion. There are no red flags; what is present is a disciplined, relationship-driven operator mid-transition from pure fleet leasing to mobility services — with technology now formalised at board level for the first time.
