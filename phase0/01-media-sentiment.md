## 1. Press Narrative Overview `DATA / ASSESSED`

Ogilvie Fleet's media presence is **predominantly positive and trade-facing** — the company generates consistent, favourable coverage in specialist fleet and leasing publications but has near-zero mainstream business press visibility. It does not appear in national newspapers, BBC, or general business media except in passing sector round-ups. This is consistent with a privately held, regionally anchored Scottish business that does not seek investor-facing publicity.

The dominant narrative across the review period can be summarised in five recurring frames:

1. **"Best-in-class independent"** — Trade press and awards bodies consistently position Ogilvie as the quality benchmark for independent (non-bank-backed) fleet lessors. The framing is that Ogilvie punches above its size in service quality relative to institutional players.

2. **"Scotland's fleet leader"** — Regional Scottish business and trade media (The Scotsman, STV Business, Scottish Business News) frame Ogilvie as the defining fleet business of Scotland. The Stirling headquarters and family ownership story anchors this identity.

3. **"Orderly succession at the top"** — The July 2025 transition from Gordon Stephen (MD to Chairman) to Nick Hardy (new MD) was covered as a well-managed, continuity-preserving leadership event, not a crisis or strategic pivot.

4. **"Ahead on EVs but honest about the challenges"** — Ogilvie has cultivated a media position as a pragmatic, evidence-based voice on fleet electrification. Gordon Stephen and Nick Hardy have both been quoted in Fleet News and Fleet World acknowledging EV residual value difficulties while framing Ogilvie's product responses (ReLease, MiSalarySacrifice) as solutions.

5. **"Award-winning every year"** — Fleet News awards coverage features Ogilvie Fleet or its people almost annually. The brand is synonymous with industry recognition in its tier.

## 4. Key Themes — Recurring Media Topics `DATA / ASSESSED`

### Theme 1: Fleet electrification leadership (recurring, dominant)
Ogilvie is consistently cited as an early and vocal advocate for fleet EV adoption. Coverage includes commentary on ZEV mandate implications, BIK tax changes, and EV residual value risk. The tone is constructive — Ogilvie is portrayed as helping customers navigate complexity, not avoiding it.

Notable product coverage:
- **MiSalarySacrifice** — salary sacrifice scheme for employee EVs; covered in Fleet News and trade HR media as a growth product and customer retention tool
- **ReLease** — used EV lease product; positioned as a practical response to EV residual value losses; covered as market innovation
- **Rental Select+** — flexible short-term rental offering; limited coverage but part of the mobility ecosystem narrative

### Theme 2: Customer retention and service quality (recurring)
The 98% customer retention figure has been cited in Fleet News and in Ogilvie's own award submissions repeatedly. Awards wins and shortlistings generate consistent positive coverage cycles.

**Fleet News Awards context:** Fleet News holds the most influential annual awards in the UK fleet sector. Ogilvie's consistent presence in shortlists and its staff members winning individual awards (Driver of the Year, Fleet Professional of the Year tiers) maintains a high media profile relative to its size.

### Theme 3: Independent operator resilience amid consolidation (emerging, increasing)
From mid-2024 onward, as GVG, Lloyds/Tusker, and Rivervale consolidation deals generated trade press coverage, Ogilvie began appearing as a named example of the "successful independent" counter-narrative. Coverage implicitly (and occasionally explicitly) contrasts Ogilvie's organic growth and retention with the integration risk carried by acquirers.

### Theme 4: Leadership succession and continuity (Jul 2025 event, ongoing)
The July 2025 leadership restructure — Gordon Stephen stepping up to Chairman, Nick Hardy becoming MD, Jill Stephen moving to Operations Director, Andy Stephen to Technology Director, Ashley Crookes joining as Sales & Marketing Director — generated the highest single-month media volume for Ogilvie in the review period. Coverage was unanimous in tone: orderly, positive, continuity-confirming.

Gordon Stephen's Hall of Fame status (Fleet News, 2021) lent retrospective weight to the transition narrative — every piece framed him as a legend stepping back, not a leader stepping down.

### Theme 5: ONWRD acquisition and mobility diversification (Dec 2025 / Jan 2026)
The acquisition of ONWRD (flexible rental business) was covered in Fleet News and Fleet World as evidence of Ogilvie's strategic intent to move beyond traditional contract hire toward a total mobility platform. This was framed as a proactive response to changing fleet customer needs. No negative coverage identified.

## 7. Therefore Statement

`[ASSESSED — HIGH CONFIDENCE]`

Ogilvie Fleet enters Q2 2026 with the strongest media-sentiment position in its recent history: an unbroken record of positive trade press coverage, an orderly leadership succession narrative still fresh in the market's memory, a product diversification story (ReLease, ONWRD, MiSalarySacrifice) that gives trade journalists something new to write about, and a competitive vacuum in Scottish fleet media following Fleet Alliance's absorption into GVG.

**Therefore:** The primary media opportunity for Ogilvie is not defensive reputation management — it is proactive share-of-voice capture in a market where the two dominant narratives (EV transition anxiety and sector consolidation) both create demand for an authoritative, independent, quality-operator voice that Ogilvie is uniquely positioned to supply.

The primary media risk is passive: if Nick Hardy does not actively cultivate Fleet News and Fleet World relationships as Gordon Stephen's successor spokesperson, Ogilvie's media salience will drift — not through adverse coverage, but through the slow erosion of quote frequency and feature placement that is the quiet penalty of spokesperson transitions in trade media.

**Recommended watch:** Monitor Fleet News Leasing Company of the Year 2026 shortlist (announcements typically Q3); monitor GVG/Fleet Alliance integration coverage for any SME market share narrative that names Ogilvie as the independent alternative; track Nick Hardy's first major Fleet News feature interview as the signal that the spokesperson transition is complete.
